\section*{Conclusion}
\ProjectTitle{} bridges the gap between record-keeping business tools and fully autonomous AI by combining specialised LLM agents with retrieval-grounded knowledge, enterprise connectors and human approval at every consequential step. Our project not only automates repetitive work across HR recruitment, marketing content, podcast guest research and enterprise integrations, but also makes every output explainable through rubric evidence and cited sources. By enforcing approval gates, PII protection and complete audit logs within a reusable workflow framework, our system helps organisations work faster while keeping people accountable for the final decisions.

\vspace{2\baselineskip}
\section*{Future Scope}
The system can be extended to additional business functions such as finance, sales and customer support; multilingual and voice-based interview support; fairness monitoring for screening outcomes; broader enterprise connectors; continuous evaluation dashboards; and stronger enterprise access-control and retention policies. These additions would improve operational reach while preserving the same governance-first design that underpins the current system.
